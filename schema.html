🔗 Relationships

people → companies
Many-to-One

Each person belongs to one company. CASCADE delete: Deleting a company removes all associated people.

collaborations → companies
Many-to-One

Multiple collaborations can involve one company. CASCADE delete: Deleting a company removes all its collaborations.

collaborations → projects
Many-to-One

Multiple collaborations can be associated with one project. CASCADE delete: Deleting a project removes all its collaborations.

collaborations → people
Many-to-One

Multiple collaborations can involve one person. CASCADE delete: Deleting a person removes all their collaboration records.

companies → people (reverse)
One-to-Many

One company can have many people working for it.

companies → collaborations (reverse)
One-to-Many

One company can be involved in many collaborations.

projects → collaborations (reverse)
One-to-Many

One project can have many collaborations.
